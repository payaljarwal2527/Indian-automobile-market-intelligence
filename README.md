# Indian Automobile Industry: Market Intelligence and Competitive Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the Indian automobile industry over FY2020-21 to FY2024-25 using secondary research and Excel-based analysis.

The objective is to understand:

- Industry growth
- Passenger vehicle trends
- Electric vehicle transition
- Competitive market structure
- Market-share movements
- Industry growth drivers and challenges

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- Microsoft PowerPoint
- Secondary Research

## Analysis Performed

### 1. Industry Analysis

Analyzed:

- Industry sales
- Year-over-year growth
- Passenger vehicle sales
- Segment-level trends
- CAGR

### 2. EV Transition Analysis

Analyzed:

- Registered EVs
- EV growth
- EV penetration
- Segment-level EV adoption

### 3. Competitive Analysis

Compared major passenger vehicle manufacturers:

- Maruti Suzuki
- Hyundai Motor India
- Tata Motors
- Mahindra & Mahindra
- Toyota Kirloskar Motor
- Kia India

Calculated:

- Market share
- Market-share change
- Competitive positioning

### 4. PESTLE Analysis

The project evaluates:

- Political
- Economic
- Social
- Technological
- Legal
- Environmental

factors affecting the Indian automobile industry.

### 5. Porter's Five Forces

The competitive environment was analyzed using:

- Competitive rivalry
- Threat of new entrants
- Supplier power
- Buyer power
- Threat of substitutes

## Key Findings

- Passenger vehicles showed strong growth over the analysis period.
- Registered EV volumes increased substantially between FY2020-21 and FY2024-25.
- EV adoption differs considerably across vehicle segments.
- The passenger vehicle market has several established competing OEMs.
- Market-share movements differed across major manufacturers.
